# Clipster Product

## Register

product

## Users

Clipster serves macOS developers and power users who repeatedly copy, find, and reuse clipboard content throughout keyboard-driven workflows. They need the menu-bar app to remain available across restarts and to make common actions immediate without demanding setup or visual attention.

## Product Purpose

Clipster is a local-first clipboard manager that makes recent and pinned clipboard content fast to retrieve, filter, paste, organize, and remove. Success means the app is reliably present when needed, behaves like a native macOS utility, and keeps clipboard history useful rather than noisy.

## Brand Personality

Fast, unobtrusive, dependable. The interaction model should feel at home beside established macOS utilities and keyboard-first tools such as Raycast.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy visual treatments, decorative motion, oversized controls, web-dashboard patterns, and custom interactions that conflict with standard macOS keyboard or accessibility conventions.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the primary clipboard workflow one action away.
2. Prefer familiar macOS conventions and predictable keyboard behavior.
3. Preserve useful context while removing duplicate or redundant information.
4. Make content type visible through meaningful previews and filtering, not noisy labels.
5. Keep all clipboard data and behavior local and dependable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support complete keyboard operation, useful VoiceOver labels, system appearance, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion preferences through native macOS controls and behaviors.
